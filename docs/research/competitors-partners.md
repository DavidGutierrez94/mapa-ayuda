## Strategic lessons (verified precedent)

1. **Handoff works through named human liaisons, not open feeds.** Haiti 2010's urgent
   reports reached the US Coast Guard via a small team with a direct line — not because
   agencies pulled from an API. → Secure named contacts at Cruz Roja / Defensa Civil /
   departmental risk councils **before** investing further in API polish. The CSV export
   exists precisely so a liaison can consume data with zero integration.
2. **Volunteer↔institution formalization is achievable**: OCHA formally activated the
   Standby Task Force (Libya 2011) — with a tiered public-vs-verified access model
   structurally identical to ours. Missing Maps was founded by MSF + Red Cross + HOT.
   The winning pitch is **surge capacity they lack**, not "a better map".
3. **Don't build missing-persons tracing.** Colombia Te Busca + Asocapitales own that
   layer. We link out (already in PRD scope).
